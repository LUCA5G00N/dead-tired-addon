# Walk-sheet: dt_bell (0.7.1, the your-turn bell)

Origin: Tim's H2 answer on the gamble2 walk (24 Nov): "helpful but could be more of an actual bell". He picked `belltollhorde` in wow.export (25 Nov). Two players in a party, both on 0.7.1.

| # | Check | Type |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | `/dt gamble sound on` on both. Start a deathroll. When it becomes your roll, you hear the Undercity bell, not the drum roll. | [G] |
| B2 | Roll quickly, so your turn comes back while the bell is still tolling. No second bell starts over the first. | [G] |
| B3 | `/dt gamble sound off`. Your turn comes round: silence, and the Roll button still glows. | [G] |
| B4 | `/dt settings`, Gambling: the Your-turn bell card says "The Undercity bell tolls when it becomes your roll." | [G] |
| H1 | Is the toll the right length for a turn, or does it outstay its welcome? | [H] |
| H2 | Can you hear it over the game, at your normal volume? (It plays on the Sound Effects channel.) | [H] |
