## Findings in full

### AUD-01 (high, card 1): after a `/reload` or relog, `/dt who` stays empty

- **Where:** `DeadTired/Roster.lua:16` (`answered`), `:61` (the reply-once guard), `:63`.
- **What goes wrong in game:** Tim and a guildie are online and have seen each other. Tim types
  `/reload` (or logs out and back in on the same character). His login HELLO goes out 10 s later,
  but the guildie's client still has `answered["<Tim's key>"] = true` from its own session, so it
  never replies. Tim's fresh `heard` table stays empty. `/dt who` prints *"this session: nobody
  yet. Give the dead a minute to rise."* until someone else logs in, and never lists the
  guildies who were already on. Card 1's G5 asks for a `/reload` before G6 checks `/dt who`,
  so the walk is likely to hit it.
- **Evidence:** `audit/tests/test_roster_reload.lua`: the baseline check passes (before the
  reload B lists A); after `L.reload`, A sends 0 replies and B prints "nobody yet"; the same
  after a relog of the same character. 3 checks red.
- **Suggested fix:** reply-once per *sender session*, not per sender. Simplest: the HELLO carries
  a per-session token (e.g. `s=<GetTime() at load, rounded>` or a random number), and
  `answered` is keyed by sender plus token. A plain HELLO from a known sender with a new token
  gets one reply. Still one exchange per pair per session.

### AUD-02 (high, card 5): after the asker's `/reload`, roll calls go unanswered

- **Where:** `DeadTired/Prep.lua:88` (`qid` starts at 0 each session), `:127` (answered per
  `asker#q`).
- **What goes wrong in game:** a raid leader runs `/dt prep check` (q=1), then reloads (raid UI
  set-up, a disconnect). Their next `/dt prep check` is q=1 again. Every sharing guildie has
  `answered["<leader>#1"]` and stays silent. The summary says *"Ready (0): none. Short (0):
  none."* It only recovers once the leader's `qid` passes the highest number used before.
- **Evidence:** `audit/tests/test_prep_reload.lua`: baseline "Ready (1): Graverobber" passes; after
  the reload the same roll call prints "Ready (0): none. Short (0): none." 1 check red.
- **Suggested fix:** make the question id unique across sessions: e.g. `q = time() % 1000000`
  (seconds; the 60 s window already prevents two in one second), still 1-6 digits so the wire
  and validation stay as they are.

### AUD-03 (high, card 6): after the asker's `/reload`, stash searches go unanswered

- **Where:** `DeadTired/Stash.lua:199` (`qid`), `:184` (answered per `asker#q`).
- **What goes wrong in game:** as AUD-02: after a `/reload`, `/dt stash linen` prints *"Nobody's
  got it. Or nobody's sharing."* although sharing guildies hold linen.
- **Evidence:** `audit/tests/test_stash_reload.lua`: baseline "Graverobber: Linen Cloth x20"
  passes; after the reload, "Nobody's got it." 1 check red.
- **Suggested fix:** as AUD-02 (`time()`-based `q`; the 30 s window keeps it unique).

### AUD-04 (high, card 4): after a restart, "Already noted" for the same enemy for days

- **Where:** `DeadTired/Gank.lua:99` stores `t = DT.now()` (= `GetTime()`) in `DeadTiredDB`;
  `:92` compares it with `GetTime()` in a later session.
- **What goes wrong in game:** `GetTime()` is the machine's uptime, not a calendar clock. Tim's
  PC has been up 5 days when he logs Backstabz. He restarts the PC; the next evening Backstabz
  ganks him again. `now - e.t` is negative, so *"Already noted. Grudges don't need two copies."*,
  nothing recorded, nothing sent, until uptime passes the old value (here, about 5 days). The
  enemies who gank you repeatedly are the ones the diary is for.
- **Evidence:** `audit/tests/test_gank_reboot.lua`: session 1 at uptime 500000 s is noted;
  session 2 at uptime 1200 s says "Already noted" and sends nothing. 2 checks red.
- **Suggested fix:** keep the double-press window in memory only (a session table
  `lastPress[who] = GetTime()`), or store `time()` (epoch) instead of `GetTime()`. Either
  makes the 300 s window mean 300 s.

### AUD-05 (medium, card 1): HELLO replies fan out with the guild's size

- **Where:** `DeadTired/Roster.lua:51-64`.
- **What goes wrong in game:** each client replies to each new sender with its own broadcast
  HELLO. Every client on the channel receives every reply, so one is enough, but each login draws
  one from every guildie online: with 20 online, one login costs 20 messages. Two clients whose
  login HELLOs cross inside 10 s answer each other twice. A wave of 20 logins within 2 s is 400
  messages in the first minute, each client queues 10, and the last reply goes out 9.7 s late
  (traffic S1b). Not a failure at 20 (nothing dropped); it is the cost that grows with the square
  of the number online, and it uses Blizzard's own per-sender allowance, whose limit on Forever
  isn't known (see live-client assumptions).
- **Evidence:** `audit/tests/test_traffic.lua`, S1 and S1b, numbers under *Traffic*.
- **Suggested fix:** answer a HELLO only if this client hasn't sent any HELLO in the last ~30 s
  (every HELLO already reaches the whole channel); that keeps "everyone online hears from
  everyone" and makes a login cost about one message per client per window.

### AUD-06 (medium, cards 1, 5, 6): a connected-realm guildie is keyed two ways

- **Where:** `DeadTired/Comms.lua:158-170` (a sender on another realm keeps
  `CHAT_MSG_ADDON`'s spelling, no spaces: `ArgentDawn-Stromham`); `Core.lua` `charKey()` and
  every key a client sends about itself use `GetRealmName()`'s spelling (`Argent Dawn-Stromham`).
  Compared at `Prep.lua:150`, `Stash.lua:222`, `Alts.lua:53` (`e.main == main`).
- **What goes wrong in game:** only for a guild that spans connected realms and a realm whose
  name has a space or hyphen. A guildie on such a realm runs `/dt prep check` or `/dt stash`: the
  answers carry `to=ArgentDawn-Stromham`, their own `charKey()` is `Argent Dawn-Stromham`, so every
  answer is dropped. `/dt alts Stromham` on the other realm prints *"Stromham-Argent Dawn's lot:
  main Stromham-Argent Dawn; alts (1): Stromham-ArgentDawn."*: the main listed as its own alt.
  Whether Forever has connected realms at launch is not known.
- **Evidence:** `audit/tests/test_realm_spelling.lua`, 2 checks red. Card 1's report describes the
  own-realm normalisation (so *partly* known); card 4's report notes the target realm format is
  unknown, but neither names the mismatch.
- **Suggested fix:** one canonical key for every character: strip spaces and hyphens from the
  realm everywhere (`charKey()`, `senderKey()`, `/dt gank`'s `who`), or carry
  `GetNormalizedRealmName()` (the probe records it) and normalise all comparisons through one
  helper. Existing stored keys need a one-time rewrite at load.

### AUD-07 (medium, card 3; same shape in cards 4, 5, 6): no inbound limit per sender

- **Where:** `DeadTired/Crypt.lua:102-114`; `Gank.lua:118-126`; `Prep.lua:122-135`;
  `Stash.lua:179-195`.
- **What goes wrong in game:** the 60 s death window lives in the *sender's* client. Any client
  that sends DEATH more often (a bug in a later version, `/run`, a modified copy) makes every
  guildie with `/dt rip` on print a line per message, and fills their graveyard. The same shape
  elsewhere: GANK floods push the receiver's own logs out of the 500 cap; a PREPQ or STASHQ with a
  fresh `q` each time makes every sharing guildie answer each one (one message, up to 19 replies).
  Nothing here crashes or leaks; it lets one client make everyone else's print, store or send.
- **Evidence:** `audit/tests/test_death_flood.lua`: 10 DEATHs from one sender in 10 s print 10
  notices and keep 10 graveyard entries. 2 checks red.
- **Suggested fix:** receiver-side limits per sender: DEATH at most one per sender per 60 s
  (matching the sender's window); GANK at most one per sender and `who` per 300 s; PREPQ one per
  asker per 60 s and STASHQ one per asker per 30 s (matching the askers' own windows).

### AUD-08 (medium, cards 5 and 6 on card 1's queue): late answers are lost without a word

- **Where:** `Prep.lua:116` and `Stash.lua:262` (summary after 10 s); `Prep.lua:150-155`,
  `Stash.lua:223` (later answers ignored); card 1's FIFO queue (`Comms.lua:101-127`).
- **What goes wrong in game:** an answer sits behind whatever its sender has queued, and is held
  entirely while the sender's addon messages are restricted. If it reaches the asker after 10 s
  it is dropped, and the summary counts only what arrived. Traffic S4: ten guildies in a
  restricted encounter, five roll calls from the others each got 9 of 19 answers; the ten answer
  ~9 minutes later, into nothing. S5 (everyone asks at once, all sharing): all 20 askers missed
  answers, worst 10 of 19. S3 (a normal raid night, roll call 90 s after the login wave) got 19
  of 19. The closing line *"Only guildies running the addon with sharing on answer."* doesn't
  cover "some answered too late".
- **Evidence:** `audit/tests/test_traffic.lua`, S3-S5 (printed numbers).
- **Suggested fix:** drop an answer from the queue rather than send it once its asker's window
  has passed (stamp it when queued), and have the summary say how many arrived late if any did
  (count late answers for one more 20 s).

### AUD-09 (medium, card 6): shared play time is never withdrawn

- **Where:** `Played.lua:75-82` (stored per sender, forever), `:122-124` (`share off` changes only
  your own flag).
- **What goes wrong in game:** a guildie shares `/played`, then thinks better of it:
  `/dt slowest share off` stops future sends, but every guildie who heard them keeps the old
  entry in `DeadTiredDB.played` and keeps ranking them on `/dt slowest` for good. The same for
  someone who leaves the guild. CLAUDE.md: play time is "shared only after the player switches
  [it] on"; switching it off should end the sharing too.
- **Evidence:** code reading; no test (medium).
- **Suggested fix:** `share off` sends one `PLAYED {off=1}`, and receivers delete that sender's
  entry; entries older than 30 days drop off the board.

### AUD-10 (medium, card 1): a refused `SendAddonMessage` counts as sent

- **Where:** `Comms.lua:109`.
- **What goes wrong in game:** `ok = pcall(send, ...)` is true whenever the call doesn't raise.
  On retail-API clients, `C_ChatInfo.SendAddonMessage` reports refusal by its return value (a
  result code for throttle, not in guild, and so on), not by raising. Such a message is counted in
  `comms.sent` and lost, with no retry. The harness stub always returns 0 (`harness.lua:110`), so
  no offline test can see it.
- **Evidence:** can't be reproduced offline: it depends on the live client's return value. The
  probe records the ping's returns (`comms.pings[i].result`), so its report covers it *partly*.
- **Suggested fix:** after the probe answers what the return looks like, treat any result other
  than success (or `true`) as a failure: count it, put the message back at the head of the queue,
  and wait as for the throttle.

### AUD-11 (medium, card 7): an unknown log state arms a toggle

- **Where:** `Logs.lua:72-78` (`combatOn ~= true` → `/combatlog`), `:235`.
- **What goes wrong in game:** if neither `C_ChatInfo.IsLoggingCombat` nor a no-argument
  `LoggingCombat()` answers (absent, erroring, secret), the state is unknown and treated as off.
  `/combatlog` toggles; if the log was in fact on (another addon or the player started it), the
  click switches it **off**. CLAUDE.md's ruled exception for card 7: "never switching a log off".
  The card's brief asked for "unknown is treated as off", so the code follows its brief, but the
  brief and the house rule disagree in this one state. Reachable only if both readers fail on
  Forever; the probe's `logging` section will say.
- **Evidence:** code reading; the report documents the behaviour, not the risk (*partly*).
- **Suggested fix:** with the state unknown, don't arm the button: show the dialog with
  *"Can't tell if the log is on. Type /combatlog if it isn't."*, or don't ask at all.

### AUD-12 (medium, all cards, tests): nobody else ever forgets

- **Where:** every two-client suite (`test_roster.lua`, `test_prep.lua`, `test_stash.lua`,
  `test_gank.lua`): none reloads one peer while the other keeps its state, and none moves
  `GetTime()` backwards across a SavedVariables round-trip.
- **What goes wrong:** the guards *reply-once*, *one answer per question* and *double-press
  window* were each proved red by tests in which both sides start fresh, so they passed for the
  case that matters least. AUD-01 to AUD-04 went through every guard proof.
- **Suggested fix:** add `L.reload()` from `audit/tests/lib.lua` to card 1's harness as
  `client:reload()`, and give each module with per-session state a "peer reloads" check.

### AUD-13 (medium, all cards, tests): the secret-value stub can't fail

- **Where:** `harness.lua:95` (`issecretvalue` looks the value up in a table); the real client's
  secrets raise on comparison, arithmetic and concatenation.
- **What goes wrong:** a test can only show that `clean()` was called where the author aimed it.
  A secret that reaches `<`, `..` or `#` elsewhere passes offline. Example of a path that relies
  on it: `Stash.lua:73-74` compares `info.itemID` and `info.stackCount` from
  `C_Container.GetContainerItemInfo` without `clean()` on the fields (only the table and
  `isBound` are cleaned). Own-bag fields are unlikely to be secret, so no player-facing finding.
- **Suggested fix:** a harness secret built with `newproxy(true)` (Lua 5.1 honours `__len` only
  on userdata), whose metatable raises on `__lt`, `__le`, `__concat`, `__len`, `__add` and
  `__index`. Its `type()` reads `"userdata"`, not the real type, but the operators are what break.

### AUD-14 (low, card 7): the instance dialog outlives the instance

- **Where:** `Logs.lua:253-259` (leaving clears the visit but doesn't hide the dialog);
  `frame()` (`:137-156`) doesn't add the dialog to `UISpecialFrames`.
- **What goes wrong in game:** zone in, ignore the dialog, hearth out: it stays on screen in
  Orgrimmar until clicked. Escape doesn't close it.
- **Suggested fix:** hide (or defer the hide in combat) when the visit ends; add both frame names
  to `UISpecialFrames`.

### AUD-15 (low, card 1): an `init` error is silent and permanent

- **Where:** `Core.lua:122` (`def.init()` unprotected), before `:123` registers the events.
- **What goes wrong in game:** if a module's `init` raises (a bad SavedVariables shape, a client
  difference), the error shows once at load, that module never registers its events for the
  session, and nothing is counted under its `errorKey`.
- **Suggested fix:** `pcall(def.init)`, count a failure under `errorKey`/`lastKey` as handlers
  do, and register the events regardless.

### AUD-16 (low, card 1): chunk ids restart after a `/reload`

- **Where:** `Comms.lua:75` (`msgid = 0` per session), `:194` (partials keyed `sender#msgid`, kept
  30 s).
- **What goes wrong in game:** nothing today: the longest message any card sends is GANK at 250
  bytes, so nothing is chunked. If a later card sends more than 250 bytes, a reload within 30 s of
  a chunked send could join slices from two different messages (`p.n` matching).
- **Suggested fix:** start `msgid` from `time() % 100000`.

## Traffic (item 4)

`audit/tests/test_traffic.lua`: 20 clients, each loading all 18 files of cards 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and
7 (module lists merged in the test, in merged `.toc` order), one fake GUILD bus, card 1's real
`Comms.lua` throttle (bucket 5, +1/s) and restricted hold (retry 5 s, cap 50). Deterministic.

| Scenario | Messages | Per minute | Peak queue (one client) | Max queueing delay | Dropped |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 login wave: 20 log in over 2 min | 237 in 10 min (all HELLO: 20 + 217 replies) | 34, 148, 55, then 0 | 0 | 0 s | 0 |
| S1b login wave: 20 log in within 2 s | 400 (20 + 380 replies) | 400 in minute 1 | 10 | 9.7 s (a HELLO reply) | 0 |
| S2 one hour, 20 online, half sharing everything | 263 | mean 4.4, busiest minute 32 | 0 | 0 s | 0 |
| S3 raid night: 20 log in within 60 s, roll call at +90 s | 289 in 10 min | 124, 165, then ~0 | 0 | 0 s | 0 |
| S4 ten clients restricted for 10 min | 320 in 15 min | ~20 while held, 160 in the minute it lifts | 16 | 530 s (held) | 0 |
| S5 all 20 run `/dt prep check` in the same second | 400 (20 PREPQ + 380 PREP) | 400 in minute 1 | 12 | 11.1 s (a PREP) | 0 |

- **S2's hour** (6 roll calls, 10 stash searches, 15 deaths, 10 ganks, 8 typed `/played`, 4
  reloads, 3 alt logins): DEATH 10, GANK 10, HELLO 70, PLAYED 5, PREP 58, PREPQ 6, STASH 94,
  STASHQ 10. Each stash search draws about 9 answers (one per sharing guildie who holds a match);
  each alt login 20 HELLOs. The 4 reloads drew no replies at all (AUD-01).
- **A login costs** 1 + (guildies online − 1) messages (AUD-05).
- **The throttle and the queue cap held**: nothing was dropped in any scenario; the largest
  queue was 16 of 50 (S4, a client restricted for 10 minutes while 6 guildies logged in and 10
  questions were asked). To reach 50, a client must be restricted while 50 HELLO-drawing logins
  and questions happen: hours of restriction at this guild size. What would be dropped first is
  the oldest queued message, which during a restriction is a HELLO reply, a prep or stash answer
  (already late, AUD-08) or the client's own DEATH.
- **Nothing multiplies beyond one hop**: no reply is ever replied to (HELLO `r=1`, PREP and STASH
  are addressed). The largest fan-outs are one HELLO → up to 19 replies (AUD-05) and one
  PREPQ/STASHQ → up to 19 answers.
- **What it lost**: answers. S4's five roll calls got 9 of 19 answers each, and S5's askers all
  missed some (worst 10 of 19) (AUD-08). S3, the realistic raid night, got 19 of 19.
- **Not modelled**: Blizzard's own addon-message limits per sender and per channel, delivery
  latency, and `SendAddonMessage` refusing a send (AUD-10). The bus delivers every message.

## SavedVariables growth (item 6)

Every list that grows is capped or bounded by the guild's size. A year of play for an active
member, estimated from each record's size as stored: gank logs 500 × ~140 B ≈ 70 KB (cap);
graveyard 100 × ~110 B ≈ 11 KB (cap); roster ~1 entry per guildie character ever heard
(~300 × 90 B ≈ 27 KB, never pruned); played board ~150 × 70 B ≈ 10 KB (never pruned, AUD-09);
stash ~100 items per character on the account; counters and `hcTold` negligible. Under 200 KB in
all. The session tables (`answered`, `heard`, `fired`, `partial`) are memory only.

## Live-client assumptions (item 8)

What the walk is really testing, beyond what the probe answers. Each is something the offline
suites stub and the live client decides.

1. **`SendAddonMessage`'s return value** and what a refusal looks like (AUD-10). The probe records
   the returns of one ping; it doesn't push the throttle.
2. **Blizzard's addon-message limits** on Forever, per sender and per GUILD channel. Card 1's
   bucket (5, +1/s) assumes they are looser.
3. **When `AreOutgoingAddonChatMessagesRestricted()` is true** on a classic-content client
   (encounters? battlegrounds? never?). S4 assumes it can last minutes.
4. **`GetTime()` is uptime and restarts with the machine** (AUD-04; retail behaviour).
5. **Secret values**: which reads are secret on Forever and that every one is `clean()`ed before
   an operator touches it (AUD-13). The probe's target section answers this for enemy names.
6. **Connected realms**: whether the guild spans more than one realm (AUD-06).
7. **`C_ChatInfo.SendChatMessage` to a `CHANNEL` needs a hardware event**: a typed `/dt recruit`
   or a macro click should count; a keybind through another addon might not.
8. **`C_GuildInfo.IsGuildOfficer()`** means "officer" as the guild's ranks define it on Forever.
9. **`Enum.BagIndex.Bank` and `BankBag_1..n`** exist as on older retail. A client with bank tabs
   instead would leave stash's bank read empty without a word (the probe records the enum's keys).
10. **`C_Container.GetContainerItemInfo(...).isBound`** is present (A4 falls back to
    `C_Item.IsBound`).
11. **The secure `/combatlog` macro button** fires once per click with
    `RegisterForClicks("AnyUp", "AnyDown")` and `ActionButtonUseKeyDown` (card 7's report names
    this; the beta addon worked on the beta).
12. **`date("!*t", t)`** is available in the client's `date`.
13. **A bare `|` in output**: `/dt` with no verb (`Core.lua` `help`) and `/dt logs` usage print
    `" | "` separators. If the chat frame takes `| ` as a broken escape, the separators vanish
    or garble; cosmetic.
14. Already named in the reports, not repeated as findings: the `CHAT_MSG_ADDON` sender format;
    late `PLAYER_GUILD_UPDATE`; `UnitName`'s target realm format; `TIME_PLAYED_MSG`'s argument
    order; `hash_SlashCmdList` caching `/played`; `GetServerTimeLocal`'s meaning;
    `GetItemCount`'s second argument; `GetInstanceInfo`'s 8th return; whether Forever has
