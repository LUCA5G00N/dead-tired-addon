**Dead Tired, the guild addon, is rising.**

It's optional. Nobody has to run it, nothing needs it, and it only stirs for <DEAD TIRED>. Quiet by default: one welcome line at login, then nothing unless you ask.

Three things it does:
- **Rest in Pieces.** When a guildie dies, you get one dry line. Your own deaths stay private until you `/dt rip share on`.
- **The gank diary.** Target the Alliance player who just ruined your evening, press `/dt gank`, and the guild's board remembers. `/dt ganks` shows who's earned the most grudges.
- **Raid prep.** `/dt prep` checks your bags against the reagent list; `/dt prep check` asks who's ready (only guildies who opted in answer).

Also: who runs it (`/dt who`), alts, a guild stash search, the slowest-leveller award, Fishing Extravaganza reminders and a nudge to switch the combat log on in dungeons. Anything personal is off until you switch it on.

Lists only show guildies running the addon, so the more of us dead, the better.
